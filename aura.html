About AURA

AURA is a sustainable fashion management concept designed to help people understand the true cost of what they wear, encourage rewearing, and make more conscious buying decisions. This project demonstrates Flexbox, Grid, HTML5 APIs, a navigation bar, a hero section, and modals - all in a single HTML page.
